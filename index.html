Frequently Asked Questions

What do you make?

We do welding and metal fabrication, including iron gates, steel and iron doors, steel railings and other custom metalwork for homes, businesses and construction projects.

Where are you located?

Our workshop is in Okpanam, Asaba, Delta State, Nigeria. You can find us on the map in the Contact section.

How do I get a quote?

Call us, message us on WhatsApp, or use the quote form below. Send the design you want, the size and a photo of the place, and we will get back to you.

Can you build my own design?

Yes. Send a picture or describe what you want and we will fabricate it to your specifications.

Do you work outside Asaba?

Contact us with your location and we will let you know if we can handle your project.
